## Tone and style rules
- Use natural Bangla understood by Bangladesh hotel owners and reception staff.
- English technical words (POS, HMS, QR) are acceptable where users commonly understand them.
- On first mention, explain unfamiliar terms in simple language.
- Avoid stiff translations, repetitive exact-match keywords and awkward transliterated terms.
- Keep buttons and labels consistent across pages and product UI.
- Distinguish **property type** (hotel/resort) from **accommodation type** (room/cottage/villa/apartment/dormitory) and **category** (deluxe/suite/twin).

## Sample
English: “View today's arrivals and room availability.”  
Bangla: “আজ কারা চেক-ইন করবে আর কোন রুমগুলো খালি আছে—এক জায়গায় দেখুন।”

English: “Which features are included in this plan?”  
Bangla: “এই প্ল্যানে কোন কোন সুবিধা পাওয়া যাবে?”

Do not translate commercial eligibility differently from source copy.
