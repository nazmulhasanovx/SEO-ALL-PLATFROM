# Translation, URL, Hreflang and Editorial QA
**Status:** Acceptance checklist; tests not executed.

## Before publishing a locale
- [ ] Confirm actual search intent in that language.
- [ ] Native editor reviews terminology, clarity and regional tone.
- [ ] Product owner confirms same availability, limits, pricing and integration statements.
- [ ] Browser/headless inspection confirms correct html lang and readable UTF-8 output.
- [ ] A valid, unique title and meta description are written for each locale.
- [ ] Self-canonical and bidirectional hreflang link only to public, live, equivalent URLs.
- [ ] Internal links and language selector work.
- [ ] Buttons, form text, dates, prices and measurement units make sense to target market.
- [ ] Screenshots are localized when necessary and do not contain personal information.
- [ ] Search Console inspect live URL and monitor index coverage over time.

## Future language expansion
Research customer acquisition and support capacity first; adding many poorly maintained machine-translated versions may reduce usefulness, create inconsistent promises and undermine brand trust.
