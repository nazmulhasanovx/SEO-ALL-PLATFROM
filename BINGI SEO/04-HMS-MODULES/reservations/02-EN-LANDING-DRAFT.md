## Make each booking easier to follow
Bookings are not isolated records: they affect arrivals, room availability, guest preparation and the front desk. BinGi's hospitality ecosystem brings reservations into the context of property operations so teams can see the next action in the guest journey.

## A clear workflow for hotel teams
1. **Booking initiated** — a reservation may begin through a supported booking source.
2. **Reservation recorded** — front desk staff can work from the hotel's reservation view, subject to permissions and plan eligibility.
3. **Arrival preparation** — the team checks booking details and room readiness.
4. **During the stay** — the reservation provides operational context as supported services are recorded.
5. **Departure and reporting** — relevant checkout and business activity can be reflected in the connected workflows.

*Exact automation and available functions depend on the production release and subscription plan. Confirm in your demonstration.*

## Who benefits?
- **Hotel owner:** better visibility into reservations and daily property activity.
- **Reception team:** one operational place to reference guest arrivals and booking context.
- **Reservation manager:** clearer overview of today's booking activity and upcoming check-ins.
