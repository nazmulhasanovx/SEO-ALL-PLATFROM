# Build Authentic BinGi Brand Authority

## First-party consistency
- Approved organization and brand spelling, legal attribution and official websites.
- Real support/sales contact, public documentation and up-to-date product description.
- Social account descriptions and business listings only for real accounts/premises.
- Consistent plan names and up-to-date screenshots, no obsolete promotional claims.
- Link each video, product guide and relevant external profile back to a real public landing page.

## Earned third-party evidence
1. Hospitality industry explainers using genuine operators' problems.
2. Approved implementation case study with results and limitations.
3. Conference/session demonstration and recording with actual product UI.
4. Practical tutorials that other hospitality businesses may voluntarily reference.
5. Editorial commentary and public interviews with permission.

## Consent and evidence
For every customer-facing mention, collect written consent for name/logo, testimonial quote, hotel photos and any disclosed business results. Store contracts/permission privately, not in this public repo.

## Avoid
Paid link networks, fake endorsements, copied reviews, mass directory spam, fake social accounts, undisclosed sponsored mentions and scraped third-party logos.

## Distribution goals
Track referral traffic, quality external mentions, relevant brand-search growth and demo requests. No link-volume or “domain authority” guarantee.
