# Official Demo Video & Case Study SEO

## One module, one practical video
1. Real hotel problem in first 20 seconds.
2. Explain the module and its supported plan/version.
3. Screen record a real workflow with fully anonymized sample data.
4. Show outcome and where the module links to another operation.
5. Mention genuine limitations and optional integrations.
6. End with a relevant official product module URL and CTA.

## Metadata structure
- Descriptive English or বাংলা title matching actual content.
- Human-written summary, chapters, subtitles and transcript.
- Relevant official landing page link, no keyword stuffing.
- Real thumbnail artwork that represents the recording accurately.
- Distinct localized video version if voice is separately recorded.
- Update or annotate older videos when a UI/feature changes.

## Case study template
**Client/property:** named only with consent  
**Context and starting situation:** verified  
**Installed BinGi modules and plan:** approved  
**Onboarding/training:** dated and factual  
**Change observed:** measured outcome and method  
**Customer quote/logo:** written permission  
**Caveats:** important operational limitations

## Starting sequence
HMS overview → Reservations → Room/Accommodation creation → Front Desk → Housekeeping → Restaurant → Staff → Finance. Only film a module once its screen and workflow have passed product QA.
