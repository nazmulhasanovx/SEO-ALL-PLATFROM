# Hotel & Resort SEO Page Briefs
**Draft:** Research and editorial direction, not published pages.

## A. Hotels / হোটেল
**Existing public landing:** https://technology.bingi.com.bd/solutions/hotels — prefer improving this URL instead of creating an unnecessary duplicate.

**Search intents:** hotel management system, hotel operations software, hotel booking and front desk; হোটেল ম্যানেজমেন্ট সফটওয়্যার, হোটেল বুকিং সফটওয়্যার।

**Proposed English H1:** “A Connected Management Workflow for Hotels”  
**Proposed Bangla H1:** “হোটেলের বুকিং থেকে চেক-আউট—একসঙ্গে ম্যানেজ করুন” (only after product proof)

### Structured narrative
1. Hotel owner's daily questions: arrivals, room status, occupancy, revenue.
2. Reception booking and arrival queue; what staff can do.
3. Front Desk → Housekeeping room-ready handoff.
4. Guest services and restaurant where included.
5. Reporting of daily events, with real scope limitations.
6. FAQ: subscription fit, setup/training, data migration, roles.
7. Product walkthrough CTA.

**Evidence to obtain:** live demo of check-in flow; staff role permission screen; housekeeping update; plan entitlement matrix.

## B. Resorts / রিসোর্ট
**Search intents:** resort management software, resort booking platform, resort cottage management; রিসোর্ট ম্যানেজমেন্ট সফটওয়্যার, কটেজ বুকিং সফটওয়্যার।

**Proposed English H1:** “Resort Operations Across Stays, Services and Teams”  
**Proposed Bangla H1:** “রিসোর্টের কটেজ, বুকিং, রেস্টুরেন্ট ও সার্ভিস—এক জায়গায়”

### Structured narrative
1. Resort setup: different types of stay, location and numbering (only verified types).
2. Inventory and reservation workflows for arriving guests.
3. Restaurants and services: tours, pool, airport transfers, if enabled.
4. Housekeeping and maintenance handoffs.
5. Guest QR/NFC touchpoints where activated.
6. Revenue + guest operational overview.
7. FAQ: cottage vs villa, add-ons, device limits, connectivity.
8. CTA: demonstrate an actual mixed-inventory resort.

**Boundary:** Internal plan includes Room/Cottage/Villa/Apartment/Dormitory/Custom; do not say all six are live before testing.

## C. Avoid identical pages
Do not substitute “hotel” for “resort” in a copy-pasted template. Each page needs distinct workflow diagrams, real screenshots and examples and specific internal links.

## Translation note
English/Bangla titles and meta descriptions should be natural, user-friendly and based on final approved product promises.
