# BinGi SEO Editorial Pipeline
**Draft, 2026-11-09**

## Workflow
1. Choose a keyword cluster from Category 03 and check actual search intent.
2. Check whether a useful approved page already exists; prefer improving it instead of creating duplicates.
3. Obtain current product evidence; do not use internal roadmaps as live feature proof.
4. Write a helpful English original with a precise answer and real examples.
5. Localize in natural বাংলা; verify all plan and integration statements match.
6. Have product owner approve claims, including price and client references.
7. Add source-backed visuals, accessibility alt text, internal links and demo CTA.
8. SEO review: unique title, description, heading, URL strategy and structured data where eligible.
9. Publish to the actual site, not just GitHub; check HTTP status, canonical, links, privacy and responsive UI.
10. Update sitemap, monitor indexing and traffic, and set next review date.

## Types of content
- **Product landing:** what the software/module actually does for a buyer.
- **Solution landing:** specific property workflows, not a duplicate of module descriptions.
- **Tutorial:** exact steps with screenshots from the supported release.
- **Comparison guide:** fair evaluation criteria, evidence, no fabricated product attacks.
- **Case study:** only with client authorization and verified results.
- **Release note:** what changed, when, plans impacted and what remains planned.

## Writing quality
Helpful headings, short paragraphs, clean tables, examples relevant to Bangladesh when justified. Avoid repetitive keyword blocks, hypothetical customer testimonials and AI-generated generic fluff. Include honest limitations.

## Quality approval metadata
Content ID; page owner; user intent; EN/BN versions; product status; evidence URLs; last verified date; final live URL; publication date; revision schedule; assigned CTA and analytics event.

## Post-publication monitoring
Check Search Console/Bing, actual query fit and conversion; improve factual clarity where needed. Log changed claims and update dependent locale pages together.
