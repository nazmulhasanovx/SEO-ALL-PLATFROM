## Information architecture rules
- One canonical guide per workflow and locale.
- A module overview links to relevant docs; a document links back to the module.
- Keep visible breadcrumbs, step numbers, update date and tested release/version.
- Show required permissions, prerequisites, limitations and recovery steps.
- Use real current interface screenshots; omit credential/session or customer data.
- Archive superseded instructions with notes and redirects to current docs.

## SEO purpose
Useful exact-answer help articles can support long-tail searches, reduce support questions and give search engines first-party context. Avoid turning every FAQ into a thin standalone page.

## Publishing gate
Technical writer drafts → product QA performs procedure → fact reviewer signs off → EN/BN editor validates → deploy → test navigation/structured data/index status.
