# Keyword Research Methodology — English & বাংলা
**Date:** 2026-11-09 | **Type:** SOP | **State:** Planning

## Research workflow
1. Identify target user and decision/problem: owner, reservations, housekeeping, operations, HR, restaurant, finance, traveler.
2. Extract terms from actual BinGi website navigation and approved release specs; avoid invented capabilities.
3. Mine Google Search Console and Bing queries for each verified domain; classify brand/non-brand.
4. Use keyword research, SERP observation, customer-call language and competitor information to discover related questions.
5. Research English, native বাংলা, and naturally used English/Banglish phrases separately.
6. Mark intent: **informational / commercial / transactional / navigational / troubleshooting**.
7. Cluster near-duplicates by intent; one high-quality page per distinct intent rather than one page per phrase.
8. Score opportunity and prioritize. Validate actual volume and difficulty when tools/data are available.
9. Map each approved query cluster to one canonical existing or proposed URL; define internal links and CTA.
10. Revisit rankings and conversions after publication.

## Sample scoring model (proposal)
Impact 0–5, product-fit 0–5, likelihood to convert 0–5, content confidence 0–5, effort 0–5.

Priority score = `(Impact + ProductFit + Conversion + Confidence) - Effort`. Do not present it as measured keyword difficulty or proprietary search volume.

## Geographic strategy
**Phase 1:** Bangladesh English and বাংলা, major travel/property intents.  
**Phase 2:** International English only where the product actually serves buyers.  
**Phase 3:** Hindi/Arabic/other locales after proper localization, sales/support readiness and demand evidence.

## Research evidence register
Fields: Query; language; location; source; observed search volume (source/date); intent; SERP competitors; planned page; product evidence; live URL; owner; last checked.

Do not fake search volume, CPC or ranking data. Avoid keyword stuffing or text spun from other sites.
