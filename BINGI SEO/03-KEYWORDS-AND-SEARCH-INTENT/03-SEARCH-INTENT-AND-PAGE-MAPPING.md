## Cannibalization prevention
- Product overview targets general HMS category; individual module pages target deep features.
- A how-to guide answers a question and links to the relevant product module, rather than repeating the sales landing page.
- Hotel vs Resort solution pages should describe distinct operational needs, not be keyword-swapped duplicates.
- English and Bangla equivalents have independent language URLs and correct hreflang.
- Similar 'price in Dhaka' and 'price in Bangladesh' terms do not justify duplicate thin pages.
- The hotel marketplace's guest SEO remains on bingi.com.bd, not duplicated as software content on the technology domain.
