# SEO Content QA, Claim Control & Versioning
**Status:** Proposed publishing standard | **2026-11-09**

## Content status
1. **DRAFT:** informational / not approved.
2. **FACT-CHECKED:** product and commercial reviewer checked claims.
3. **SEO-REVIEWED:** intent, canonical, title, metadata, links, locale and accessibility reviewed.
4. **APPROVED:** permission to publish.
5. **PUBLISHED:** exact live URL verified by QA.
6. **MONITORED:** analytics and freshness checks active.
7. **ARCHIVED:** superseded but history preserved.

## Capability status
- **LIVE VERIFIED:** published with evidence, plan scope and limitations.
- **PILOT/LIMITED:** say clearly which segment and conditions.
- **PLANNED:** write “planned” or “in development”; not sold as live.
- **UNVERIFIED:** omit from promotional claims until evidence is provided.

## Every future content brief needs
Content ID; module; target audience; primary search intent; EN/BN text; proposed/existing URL; publication state; evidence; factual owner; last update; primary query/topic; internal links; screenshots provenance; CTA; structured data eligibility; translation/hreflang counterpart.

## Content quality checklist
- [ ] Solves a real question with original, helpful detail.
- [ ] Product functionality matched to approved release and plan.
- [ ] No false pricing, customer proof, OTA, device or security claims.
- [ ] Avoids near-duplicate pages targeting slight keyword variants.
- [ ] Bangla reads naturally to a Bangladeshi hotel operator.
- [ ] Mobile performance / accessibility / canonical / href language parity checked.
- [ ] CTA, contact details and internal links tested.
- [ ] Customer, staff, guest and business data sanitized.
- [ ] No fabricated stats, search-ranking guarantees, AI citations or testimonials.
- [ ] Source checked and next revision date assigned.

## Conflict resolution
Official site vs product plan disagree? Mark the item DISPUTED and hold publication; ask product owner to select the authoritative version. Git history preserves earlier versions. Record major decisions in Category 15 changelog. Keep legacy content, or archive with redirects; never silently delete.
