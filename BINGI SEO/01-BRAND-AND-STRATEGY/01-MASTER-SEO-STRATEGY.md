# BinGi — Multilingual SEO + AI Search Visibility Strategy
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-09 | **Status:** PROPOSED

## Vision
Make genuine, up-to-date BinGi hospitality technology information findable through Google, Bing, and AI-assisted search. Search visibility cannot be guaranteed in any engine or LLM.

**বাংলা:** হোটেল/রিসোর্ট ম্যানেজমেন্ট সফটওয়্যার খোঁজা মানুষ যেন BinGi-এর মডিউল, কাজ, সুবিধা, সীমাবদ্ধতা ও সঠিক মূল্য-সংক্রান্ত তথ্য সহজে খুঁজে পায়।

## Audience and search intent
| Buyer | Search need | Recommended content | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotel owner | Hotel management system in Bangladesh | Overview, comparison criteria, verified features | Demo request |
| Resort manager | Resort booking + operations software | Resort workflows and services | Consult sales |
| Reception / front desk | Room calendar, arrivals, departures | Reservation and operations guides | Watch demo |
| Housekeeping lead | Room status / task management | Housekeeping guide | Demo |
| Restaurant manager | Hotel restaurant POS and QR order | POS and service integrations | Demo |
| HR manager | Staff attendance, leave and payroll | HRM limits, compatible hardware evidence | Feature inquiry |
| Finance owner | Occupancy, revenue, reporting | Report screenshots and capabilities | Demo |
| Guest / traveler | Stay / hotel booking in destination | Marketplace property and destination pages | Booking |

## Domain responsibility
- **B2B: https://technology.bingi.com.bd/** — Software, ecosystem, HMS modules, solutions, pricing, support and case studies.
- **B2C: https://bingi.com.bd/** — Guest-facing accommodation, properties, destinations and booking intent.
- Keep admin dashboards and private operational data outside public indexing.
- Avoid duplicate articles across domains; link meaningfully between them.

## Proposed information architecture (NOT existing URLs)

technology.bingi.com.bd/
  en/   bn/

First inventory the existing pages and redirects. Don't change known good URLs without a 301, canonical, internal link and hreflang migration plan.

## Topic cluster priorities
**P1:** HMS platform, Reservations, Rooms & Accommodation, Front Desk/Operations, Housekeeping, Guests, Website Booking, Restaurant/POS, Staff/HRM, Finance/Reports.

**P2:** QR/NFC ordering, Services, Inventory, Communications, Staff App, Guest App, Analytics, Multi-property, hotel/resort/cottage/villa use cases.

**P3, conditional:** OTA APIs, advanced automation/AI, integrations and enterprise claims. Only publish evidence-backed details.

For each approved module, build one **canonical landing page per language**, associated FAQs/tutorials, internal links, verified screenshots, role-specific benefits, scope limitations and a suitable CTA.

## Three connected disciplines
1. **SEO:** technical discoverability, clear search intent, quality content, authoritative references.
2. **AEO:** short, directly useful answers to specific buyer questions; appropriate FAQs and guides.
3. **GEO / AI discovery:** factual entity descriptions, original evidence, accessible citations, up-to-date public documentation and monitored answer accuracy.

No AI service promises to cite or recommend a particular brand. Never use deceptive AI-spam, keyword stuffing or fabricated testimonials.

## English + Bangla rule
Create natural English originals and fully localized Bangla equivalents; test native Bangla-script and English/Banglish queries. Use separate indexable language URLs, accurate html lang attributes, and reciprocal hreflang only between genuine equivalents. Other languages follow actual market demand and editorial resources.

## Success measures
Primary: qualified demo requests and attributed organic sales leads.
Secondary: indexed pages, non-branded queries, organic CTR, engaged visits, traffic by language, page quality, factual freshness, AI answer citations sampled with date/query/engine/source.

## Publication workflow
Product source → evidence review → EN draft → BN localization → SEO/UX QA → owner sign-off → website publish → index/sitemap checks → measure and refine.
