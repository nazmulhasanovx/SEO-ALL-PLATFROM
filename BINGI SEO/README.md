## Mandatory product-claim policy

Every publishable statement must be labeled internally:

- **VERIFIED LIVE:** supported by an accessible live page, demonstrated build or product-owner-approved evidence.
- **PILOT / LIMITED:** available only in named limited deployments; scope must be stated.
- **PLANNED:** roadmap item, never marketed as an existing capability.
- **UNVERIFIED:** requires evidence and cannot be published as fact.

Prices, OTA integrations, device support, AI features, supported payment methods, client counts, platform compatibility and customer endorsements **must not be invented**. Web copy may lag product changes; obtain owner approval before publish.

## Working sequence

1. Record the facts and scope for one category.
2. Research search intent and competitor language for that category.
3. Draft in English, then independently localize in Bangla.
4. Confirm product facts and review SEO, internal linking and accessibility.
5. Publish **only after human approval**, inspect actual URLs and update the sitemap.
6. Track results and preserve revisions.
