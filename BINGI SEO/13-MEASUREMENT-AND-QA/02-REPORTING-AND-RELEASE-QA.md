# SEO Report & QA Operating Procedure
**State:** Draft.

## Weekly review
1. Priority pages: HTTP/robots/canonical/sitemap changes.
2. Search Console top queries by English and বাংলা segment.
3. Newly published pages vs actually indexed and clicked.
4. Broken links, conversions, form errors and page regressions.
5. New content freshness conflicts with latest product release/pricing.

## Monthly review
- Organic demo leads vs previous period; no false attribution.
- Non-branded impression/click trend; choose meaningful query clusters.
- Pages with traffic but low engagement/low useful conversions.
- The 10 AI prompt-suite answers, citation validity and misinformation.
- Current product capability review; update old articles and cross-language copies.
- Search generative AI reporting, if accessible for the verified Google property.
- SEO technical backlog priority, owner and verified fix status.

## QA release checklist
- [ ] All public routes 200 and navigable.
- [ ] Unique metadata, self-canonical and correct hreflang.
- [ ] HTML content rendered for crawlers.
- [ ] XML sitemap includes only intended URLs.
- [ ] Claims/source/price/plan validated by product owner.
- [ ] Responsive and accessible UI verified.
- [ ] Conversion tracking fires with consent and without PII.
- [ ] Event and spam deduplication verified.
- [ ] Published URL, product version and date saved.
- [ ] Rollback steps and changelog recorded.

## Incident handling
For leaked customer data, immediately remove/secure source access, work through incident response and do not rely on robots.txt alone. For inaccurate pricing/features, correct affected landing/FAQ/schema/locale pages and update source-of-truth metadata.

## Dashboard fields
host, language, content cluster, page group, clicks, impressions, CTR, demo leads, qualified leads, key technical issue, AI citation sample, factual review due.
